This Privacy Policy explains how the Ghost Hotel mobile application (the “App”) processes information. It is written for users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and is intended to describe the App’s actual features. It is not a substitute for advice from a qualified privacy lawyer.

1. Who is responsible

The publisher identified above is the data controller for processing carried out for Ghost Hotel. Google acts as an independent or separate provider for some Firebase Analytics and Google Mobile Ads processing under its own terms and privacy disclosures.

2. Information processed

Gameplay stored locally. The App stores progress on the device, such as completed rooms, unlocked levels, stars, keys, hints, hearts, assignments, daily progress, events, achievements, settings, language and an unfinished level. This is not a Ghost Hotel account and is not uploaded by the App.

Analytics and technical information. When Firebase Analytics is configured, the App sends gameplay events such as tutorial progress, level starts and results, mistakes, hints, achievements, virtual-currency changes and sharing actions. Google may also process app-instance identifiers, device and app information, approximate location, session information and diagnostic information according to its documentation. The App does not intentionally send your name, email address, contacts, precise location, message contents or gameplay screenshots as analytics event parameters.

Advertising information. Optional rewarded ads are provided through Google Mobile Ads. Google and its advertising partners may process advertising identifiers, device/app information, IP address, approximate location, ad requests, impressions and interactions, subject to your choices and Google’s policies.

Sharing. When you choose to share a hotel or Daily Challenge card, the App creates a PNG and text message and opens the operating system share sheet. The recipient and the selected sharing service process that information under their own policies. The App does not choose the recipient.

Permissions. The App plays short sound effects and may use vibration when enabled. It does not need the microphone to play the game and does not intentionally record audio.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Local gameplay storage is necessary to provide the game and save progress. Analytics are used to measure stability, tutorial completion, level difficulty, feature use and retention; the controller should select and document the applicable legal basis for the launch market, such as consent where required or a documented legitimate interest assessment where lawful. Advertising is used to provide optional rewarded ads and measure delivery; personalized advertising is based on consent where required by law and Google’s policies. Sharing is performed because you requested it.

We do not use the App to make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you.

4. Consent and advertising readiness

The current codebase contains a consent API but does not yet call it, and the current App configuration does not include a complete Google-certified consent-management flow. Until that is implemented and tested, personalized ads and analytics consent handling must not be represented as fully configured for users in the EEA, UK or Switzerland. The publisher must configure Google’s Privacy & Messaging/CMP flow, non-personalized or limited ads fallback, consent withdrawal and the required privacy links before release in those regions.

On iOS, any use of data for tracking also requires the applicable App Tracking Transparency permission and accurate App Store privacy disclosures.

5. Recipients and international transfers

Information may be processed by Google Firebase Analytics, Google Mobile Ads, their service providers and the sharing application you select. These providers may process information outside your country. The relevant provider’s safeguards and contractual arrangements apply to those transfers. See Google’s Privacy Policy and the applicable product disclosures.

6. Retention and deletion

Local gameplay data remains on the device until you reset progress, clear the App’s storage or uninstall the App. The publisher does not currently maintain a Ghost Hotel account database. Analytics and advertising providers retain data according to their own policies and configuration; the publisher must set and document retention periods in the relevant Firebase and AdMob consoles.

If you contact the publisher, your correspondence and contact details are kept only as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request, comply with legal obligations and establish or defend claims.

7. Your rights

Subject to legal conditions and exceptions, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, portability, or object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing. You may withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent; withdrawal does not affect earlier processing. Because gameplay is stored locally, you can usually delete it through Reset progress or by uninstalling the App.

Send requests to pronka.im@gmail.com. We may need information to verify the request. We will normally respond within one month. You may also complain to the data-protection authority in the country where you live or work. If the controller is established in Poland, the relevant authority is the Polish Data Protection Authority (UODO).

8. Children

Ghost Hotel is not knowingly directed to children below the applicable digital-consent or store age, and the App does not knowingly request names, email addresses or contacts from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact the publisher at the address above.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when the App, its providers or applicable law changes. The effective date above will be updated and material changes will be communicated through the App or its store listing where appropriate.
