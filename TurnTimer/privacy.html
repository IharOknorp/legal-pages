Privacy Policy

App: Turn Timer - Local Multiplayer
Effective date: May 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Turn Timer - Local Multiplayer ("the App") handles information when you use the App.

Summary

Turn Timer - Local Multiplayer is designed as a local board game timer. The core game data is stored on your device. The App does not require an account and does not run a developer-operated game server.

The App uses third-party services for advertising and in-app purchase management: Google AdMob, RevenueCat, and Apple App Store services.

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App may store your profile display name, player names, timer settings, saved game drafts, recent completed sessions up to 5 sessions, and a local cache of whether ads have been removed.

This information is stored in the App's local storage on your device. We do not operate a server that receives or stores this game data.

Nearby Multiplayer

If you use "Play with nearby iPhones," the App uses Apple's MultipeerConnectivity framework to discover and connect nearby devices over local network technologies such as Wi-Fi and Bluetooth.

During a nearby session, the App may share your device display name, player names, game setup settings, current timer state, turn commands, and session summary information with connected devices in the same local game session.

Nearby multiplayer data is exchanged directly between connected local devices. The App requests encrypted MultipeerConnectivity sessions. This data is not sent to a developer-operated server.

Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to show banner ads unless ads have been removed through an in-app purchase.

Google AdMob may collect or receive device and app information, advertising identifiers or similar identifiers, IP address and approximate location derived from IP address, ad impressions, clicks, performance information, diagnostics, and fraud-prevention information.

Google may use this information to provide ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and abuse, and, where permitted, personalize ads.

Purchases and RevenueCat

The App uses RevenueCat to manage the "Remove Ads" in-app purchase and to check whether the remove-ads entitlement is active.

RevenueCat may process purchase-related information, including app user identifiers generated or used by the RevenueCat SDK, product identifiers, purchase status, entitlement status, store transaction information, and device and app information used to provide the purchase service.

RevenueCat does not process your payment card details for this App. Payments are handled by Apple through App Store In-App Purchase.

Apple App Store and Payments

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. Apple may process information related to your Apple Account, payment method, purchase history, taxes, refunds, and App Store operations.

Apple Privacy Policy

Permissions

The App may request Local Network permission to discover and connect nearby iPhones for local multiplayer sessions, and Bluetooth permission to support nearby discovery and connection through Apple's local connectivity technologies.

The App does not request access to contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise GPS location for its core timer functionality.

Children

The App is intended for general board game timer use and is not directed specifically to children under 13. If you are under the age required by law in your country to use apps or make purchases, you should use the App only with permission and supervision from a parent or guardian.

Data Retention

Local game data remains on your device until it is overwritten, removed by the App, or deleted when you uninstall the App. Recent completed sessions are limited to up to 5 sessions. Third-party services such as Google, RevenueCat, and Apple retain data according to their own policies.

Your Choices

You can edit or remove player names in the App, delete the App to remove locally stored App data from your device, use "Restore Purchases" to restore a previous remove-ads purchase, and manage advertising and privacy settings through iOS settings and available Google and Apple privacy controls.

International Processing

Third-party providers used by the App may process information in countries other than your country of residence. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies and applicable law.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date and publish the updated policy.
