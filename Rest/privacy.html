Privacy Policy

Rest app · Last updated: May 18, 2026

Summary

Rest works offline and requires no account. We don't collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or use advertising identifiers. The only data we see is anonymized purchase activity routed through RevenueCat — needed to make paid Premium unlock work correctly.

1. Who we are

Rest is a mobile app for ambient sound mixes and sleep / focus timers. Developer: Ihar Pronka, contact: support@example.com.

This policy describes what data the Rest iOS app processes and exactly how.

2. What data the app collects

2.1. Data that stays on your device

The following data never leaves your iPhone, is not sent to our servers, and is not shared with any third party:

  • App settings: timer length, fade-out duration, bedtime reminder time, integration toggles.
  • Volumes and slider state in the Mixer.
  • Saved custom mixes (name, chosen cover, layer composition).
  • Onboarding completion flag.

This data is stored in standard iOS storage: UserDefaults and a local SwiftData store. It is automatically deleted when you remove the app.

2.2. Purchase-related data

To deliver paid Premium access, we use the RevenueCat service. It receives:

  • An anonymous user identifier (UUID) generated by the device. It is not tied to your name or Apple ID.
  • Information about a purchase, restoration, and active entitlement period.
  • App metadata (version, App Store country, anonymized device type).

RevenueCat processes this data as an independent data processor. Their privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

2.3. Health data (Apple Health)

If you enable the Apple Health integration in Settings, Rest will write a sleep session to the Sleep Analysis category when a nighttime timer completes. This data is stored in Apple Health's secure container on your device and is never sent to our servers.

You can disable the integration anytime in Rest's Settings or revoke permission via the Health app. The app does not read sleep data.

2.4. Notifications

If you turn on "Daily reminder" in Settings, the app will request notification permission and schedule a local notification at your chosen time. Notifications are scheduled on-device and are not delivered through any server.

3. What we don't collect

Rest does not access:

  • Location (GPS).
  • Camera or microphone. All sounds are synthesized or bundled with the app — nothing is recorded.
  • Contacts, photos, or files outside the app.
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA), tracking IDs, ATT.
  • Behavioral analytics, click events, third-party analytics SDKs.

4. Purposes of data processing

  • To remember your settings across launches.
  • To activate and validate Premium access via the App Store and RevenueCat.
  • To write sleep sessions to Apple Health — only if you enabled it.
  • To deliver local bedtime reminders — only if you enabled them.

5. Sharing data with third parties

  • Apple Inc. — payment processing and receipt validation (handled by the App Store, not initiated by us).
  • RevenueCat, Inc. — subscription state and purchase identification.

No data is shared with any other third party. We do not sell data.

6. Retention and deletion

Local data is retained while the app is installed. Removing the app from your device automatically deletes all local storage, including your presets and settings.

RevenueCat data can be deleted on request via our support address: support@example.com. We will forward the request to RevenueCat and confirm completion.

7. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13. We deliberately do not collect any data that could identify a child.

8. Your rights

Where applicable law gives you these rights (GDPR in the EU, CCPA in California, and similar laws), you may:

  • Access information about data we hold.
  • Request correction or deletion.
  • Withdraw previously granted consent (Apple Health, notifications).
  • Lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority in your jurisdiction.

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@example.com.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact
